# War of Emperium (2008)

Goro treats the legacy `AgitZone` map property from packet `0x0199` as GvG and
siege state. It deliberately does not treat it as ranked PvP: the PvP counter
and rankings remain exclusive to the corresponding PvP map properties.

## Client behavior

- Characters from another guild, guildless characters, and enemy homunculi
  can be selected for attacks and hostile skills. Members of the local guild
  cannot. Alliance and party combat restrictions remain server-authoritative,
  matching the 2008 client behavior.
- Guild masters can request an alliance or declare hostility from another
  guild member's context menu. Incoming alliances use a confirmation dialog.
  Alliances and antagonists are listed in the Guild Info tab; a guild master
  can right-click a row to remove the relation.
- Guild online-state packets update the member list and its online count.
  Guild position packets maintain a revisioned minimap marker set, cleared on
  map changes and member logout without adding per-frame allocation work.
- Legacy and formatted broadcasts appear in the console and in the centered
  announcement overlay for 20 seconds. Legacy color prefixes and the formatted
  packet's color, bounded font size, normal/bold weight, and vertical position
  are honored. Announcements are never redirected into an open public chat-room
  transcript.
- Guild emblems are requested through the existing emblem cache and shown over
  guilded actors during siege. Hovered player name labels remain available, as
  in the 2008 client. Combat damage numbers are hidden during siege, while miss
  and HP/SP recovery feedback remains visible.

Castle ownership, schedules, Emperium and guardian rules, FE/SE objectives,
death/respawn, damage reductions, investment, treasure, and castle NPCs are
server mechanics. Goro consumes their ordinary actor, model, combat, map, NPC,
guild, and announcement packets rather than duplicating those rules client-side.

## Live verification

For the default rAthena `PACKETVER=20080910` setup, test at least one first-
edition castle such as `prtg_cas01` and one second-edition castle such as
`arug_cas01`. Start and stop the respective siege modes with the server's
`@agitstart`/`@agitend` and `@agitstart2`/`@agitend2` commands.

Use characters from two guilds, plus two characters in the same guild, to
verify enemy/same-guild targeting, homunculus targeting, guild emblems,
announcements, hidden damage, misses and healing, minimap movement, alliance
accept/reject/removal, opposition, Emperium/guardian interaction, capture and
respawn, guild skills, and the castle manager/investment/Kafra/treasure NPCs.
